Application form

Tell us about you and your studies.

Fields marked with an asterisk are required. Sharing only what you are comfortable with is fine — our team can follow up for anything else.

Supporting documents may be requested after review. We never ask for payment to apply.

Who can apply

  • Secondary or tertiary students in Remo land
  • Students facing genuine financial hardship
  • Students committed to staying in school and learning
  • Indigenes and non-indigenes residing in Remo

What to expect

  1. 1

    We review every application with care and confidentiality.

  2. 2

    Our team may contact you or your school for more information.

  3. 3

    If we can help, we agree on the support and next steps with you.

Need help applying?

Email our team and we will guide you through the process.
