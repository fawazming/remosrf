What impact looks like

Support at the moment it matters most.

We focus on the barriers that cause students to drop out — and respond with practical, dignified support.

01 · Stay in school

Keeping students in the classroom.

Scholarships, bursaries and emergency relief help students remain in school when money runs out or a family crisis strikes.

  • •School fees and upkeep
  • •Learning materials
  • •Rapid relief in hardship

02 · Open doors

From examination hall to university.

JAMB, WAEC and NECO support, preparatory classes, textbooks and admission guidance remove the next financial hurdle.

  • •Exam registration
  • •Preparation and textbooks
  • •Admission guidance

03 · Build skills

Ready for a changing world.

The Mobile Digital Class, STEM training and mentorship give students skills, confidence and direction beyond the classroom.

  • •Digital literacy and laptops
  • •Coding, robotics and AI
  • •Mentorship and career guidance

Aligned with the SDGs

Local action, global goals.

Our interventions are structured around the Sustainable Development Goals — because education and decent work are the foundation of a stronger community.

SDG 4

Quality Education

Promoting quality education for students in Remo, in line with SDG 4.

SDG 8

Decent Work & Growth

Facilitating skill acquisition, entrepreneurship and employability in line with SDG 8.

Inclusive by design

We support both indigene and non-indigene students residing within Remo land, without discrimination.
