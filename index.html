Our vision

A generation of students who are intellectually sound, morally upright, skilled, and purpose-driven.

Who we are

A community answer to a familiar problem.

Remo Students Relief Foundation is a non-profit organisation based in Remo, Ogun State, Nigeria. We exist for one reason: no child in Remo should be denied education because of poverty.

Since 2024, we have supported bright but disadvantaged students with scholarships, bursaries, examination support, mentorship and digital skills — helping them stay in school, gain admission, and build a better future.

Scholarship that removes the barrier

Fees, bursaries and exam registration for students who have the ability but not the means.

Digital skills for a changing world

A solar-powered mobile classroom, STEM clubs and hands-on technology training.

Mentorship that goes the distance

Career guidance, seminars and one-on-one support for academics, character and leadership.

Impact

Over 50 students. Countless possibilities.

Between 2025 and 2026, more than 50 students directly benefited from our scholarships, bursaries, JAMB support, seminars and digital skills training. Behind each number is a student who stayed in school.

We intervene early — with fees, guidance and skills — so students can finish school, enter university, and become responsible leaders in their communities.
