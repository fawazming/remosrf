How support works

From application to a student's turning point.

A simple, human process — designed to protect dignity and get support to the right students.

1

Reach out

A student, parent, teacher or community member contacts us or completes an application.

2

Review with care

Our team reviews each situation with discretion and may request supporting documents.

3

Support begins

We provide fees, exam registration, materials, digital learning or mentorship based on need.

4

Stay connected

Students and families stay in touch with our team, and donors receive updates on the impact.
