Core Services & Programs

*Scholarship & Empowerment for Remo Youths*

At Remo Students Relief Foundation, we turn compassion into opportunity. We provide educational relief, digital skills, and mentorship to indigent students in Remo, so no child is denied education because of poverty.


1. Scholarships & Bursaries
Full and partial scholarships, school fees, and upkeep support for deserving secondary and tertiary students in Remo.

2. Examination Support
JAMB, WAEC and NECO registration support, preparatory classes, textbooks, and admission guidance — from first application to university admission.

3. Mobile Digital Class
Our solar-powered mobile classroom brings laptops, internet skills, and digital learning directly to rural schools and communities without computer access.

4. STEM Education
Hands-on training in Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics — including Coding, Robotics, and AI — through bootcamps, clubs, and competitions.

5. Mentorship, Seminars & Career Guidance
Seminars, career talks, counselling, and one-on-one mentorship to guide students on academics, character, leadership, and future careers.

6. School & Community Support
Learning materials, library books, teacher training, and enrolment drives for out-of-school children, in partnership with schools, parents, and community leaders.

7. Emergency Student Relief
Swift support for students at risk of dropping out due to financial hardship, loss of a parent/guardian, or crisis.

8. Sponsorship & Partnership
Sponsor a student, adopt a school, or partner with us on a CSR project. We provide transparent reporting and full impact updates to all donors and partners.

Who We Are

Remo Students Relief Foundation is a non-profit organisation based in Remo, Ogun State, Nigeria. We exist for one reason: _no child in Remo should be denied education because of poverty._

We support bright but disadvantaged students with scholarships, bursaries, examination support, mentorship, and digital skills — helping them stay in school, gain admission, and build a better future.

*Head Office:* 24 Abiodun Ogunyanwo Street, Makun, Sagamu, Ogun State, Nigeria.

Our Vision

To build a generation of students in Remo land who are intellectually sound, morally upright, skilled, and purpose-driven.

Our Mission

To identify, address, and strategically respond to students' educational, behavioral, and skills-related challenges through structured interventions aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Our Brief History

The Foundation was founded in 2024 in Remo out of a deep concern for the growing number of talented students dropping out of school due to financial hardship.

In communities across Sagamu, Ikenne, Remo North and beyond, many students were forced to abandon their education after secondary school — not because they lacked ability, but because they lacked support for school fees, JAMB registration, textbooks, and learning materials.

What began as a small community initiative providing bursaries to a handful of students has grown into a structured organisation committed to holistic empowerment.

The Foundation was founded by:

- _Dr. Ahmad Babatunde Asundina_
- Mr. Muritala Abdulfatai Tunde
- _Associate Professor Semiu Musa Bello (PhD)_
- _Chief Imam Sharafadeen Adeyemi_
- _Associate Professor Ganiu Yunusa (PhD)_

Between 2025 and 2026, _over 50 students_ have directly benefitted from our scholarships, bursaries, JAMB support, seminars, and digital skills training.

Description of the Foundation

Remo Students Relief Foundation is a non-profit organisation based at 24 Abiodun Ogunyanwo Street, Makun, Sagamu, Ogun State, Nigeria. Founded by Dr. Ahmad Babatunde Osundina, Mr. Muritala Abdulfatai Tunde, Associate Professor Semiu Musa Bello (PhD), Chief Imam Sharafadeen Adeyemi and Associate Professor Ganiu Yunusa (PhD), the Foundation was established to support indigent but brilliant students who risk dropping out of school due to financial challenges.

Starting with bursaries and educational support, the Foundation has grown to provide scholarships, JAMB support, seminars, mentorship, and STEM training through its innovative Mobile Digital Class. Between 2025 and 2026, over 50 students have benefitted. Its mission is simple: Scholarship & Empowerment for Remo Youths.
