Our story

It started with a simple, uncomfortable question.

Why should a brilliant student drop out of school because her family cannot afford school fees? Why should a young man with the ability to become an engineer lose his place because JAMB registration is out of reach?

In communities across Sagamu, Ikenne, Remo North and beyond, we saw that question repeating itself. Many students were forced to abandon their education after secondary school — not because they lacked ability, but because they lacked support for school fees, textbooks and learning materials.

Remo Students Relief Foundation was founded in 2024 out of a deep concern for those students. What began as a small community initiative providing bursaries to a handful of students has grown into a structured organisation committed to holistic empowerment.

We support bright but disadvantaged students with scholarships, bursaries, examination support, mentorship and digital skills — helping them stay in school, gain admission and build a better future.

“No child in Remo should be denied education because of poverty.”

Vision & mission

Our vision

To build a generation of students in Remo land who are intellectually sound, morally upright, skilled, and purpose-driven.

Our mission

To identify, address, and strategically respond to students' educational, behavioral, and skills-related challenges through structured interventions aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 4 · Quality Education SDG 8 · Decent Work & Economic Growth Remo land · Ogun State

Brief history

From a handful of bursaries to a structured movement.

A short walk through the milestones that shaped the Foundation — and the students at the centre of every one.

  1. 2024

    The Foundation is born.

    Five Remo professionals and community leaders come together after seeing talented students drop out for lack of fees, textbooks and learning materials.

  2. 2025

    From bursaries to a movement.

    Scholarships, JAMB support, seminars and mentorship begin reaching the first cohorts of students across Remo land.

  3. 2025 – 2026

    Over 50 students supported.

    Direct support expands through scholarships, bursaries, examination support, seminars and digital skills training — with more than 50 students benefiting.

  4. Today

    A holistic programme.

    The Mobile Digital Class, STEM bootcamps and structured mentorship align our work with SDG 4 and SDG 8 — supporting the whole student, not only the school bill.

  5. Next

    Deeper reach, stronger futures.

    More schools, more mentors and more students empowered to finish school, enter university and become responsible leaders.

Why Remo needs us

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not.

Many families in Remo struggle to afford education. Without support, brilliant students drop out, lose confidence, and lose their future. We intervene early — with fees, guidance and skills — so they can finish school, enter university, and become responsible leaders.

  • Fees, textbooks and exam registration for students who cannot afford them.
  • Digital skills and STEM exposure for schools without computer access.
  • Mentorship that builds character, confidence and purpose.
